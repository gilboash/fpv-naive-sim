What it is

NacoSim is a small rate-mode quad simulator that runs on the radio itself — an EdgeTX Lua script, no computer or cable needed once it is installed. You fly it with your real sticks and your own Betaflight rates: a wireframe view, a race track with gates, a lap timer and a beep on every gate. The picture is crude (it has to fit a 128×64 black and white screen); the point is stick feel and rates.

Developed and tested on a Jumper T14 with EdgeTX 2.10. Other radios are untested; there is a short list of things worth checking below.

Install

  1. Download the zip above and unzip it. You get a SCRIPTS folder and a README.
  2. Put the radio in USB Storage mode (or take the SD card out) so the SD card shows up as a drive on your computer.
  3. Copy the SCRIPTS folder onto the SD card, merging with the one already there. Afterwards you should have SCRIPTS/TOOLS/NacoSim.lua and a folder SCRIPTS/NacoSim/.
  4. Eject the card, then open NacoSim from the radio's Tools menu.

To update, copy the new SCRIPTS folder over the old one. Your settings are kept; if a new version changes how settings are stored, it migrates yours.

Flying

  • Sticks are read as set up in your model. Bring the throttle to zero to arm (THR DOWN is shown until you do), then take off.
  • ENTER respawns the quad. EXIT leaves the sim.
  • MENU (or a PAGE key, or a tap on a touch radio) opens the settings. The quad pauses while it is open.
  • A race is 3 laps by default, timed from your first pass through gate 1 to the pass that completes the last lap. The race clock is top right, the last lap bottom right. A beep marks each gate and a long beep the finish, followed by a result screen with the total and every lap.
  • A yellow diamond marks the next gate when it is on screen. When it is off to the side or behind you, an arrow at the edge of the screen points to it.

Settings

  • Sound — gate and lap beeps on or off.
  • Map — the track. There is one for now.
  • Laps — 1, 3, 5 or 10.
  • Tilt — camera up-tilt, 0 to 60 degrees.
  • Rates — presets (Quad, BF def, Soft) or Edit rates to set rate, super rate and expo for roll, pitch and yaw. The bottom line shows the resulting maximum deg/s per axis. Type in the rates you fly on your real quad and the sticks feel the same.
  • Info — radio, EdgeTX version, screen size and speed.

Settings are saved on the SD card when you close the menu.

Helping test on another radio

If you try it on a radio other than a T14, please open Settings → Info and note what it says, then tell us:

  • Does it start, and does the picture fit your screen with nothing cut off?
  • Do MENU, the wheel or keys, ENTER and EXIT all work? Which key opens the settings?
  • Is it smooth? Info shows frames per second and milliseconds per frame.
  • Anything that looks wrong or stops.

Two files on the SD card help find problems: LOGS/nacosim.csv (a flight log) and LOGS/nacosim_last.txt (the last state, and any error). Send them with your report to @nacofpv or open an issue on GitHub.

Colour radios use a separate drawing path that has only been tried against a simulated screen, not real hardware. Expect rough edges there, and please report them.
